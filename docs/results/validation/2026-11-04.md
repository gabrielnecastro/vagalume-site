# Validation round — 2026-11-04

[← Specification index](../../README.md) · [Validation reports](README.md)

Validation of the fixes for the decisions of 2026-11-04: Q-01 and Q-03 (see [global rules](../../product/global-rules.md#decided-questions)), dates in Brazil time (N-15), the legacy job address (N-24), the average rounding (BR-29), one review per job (BR-33), and the date format of the browser, and of the updated QA guide. Checked against the running app on `localhost:3000`, through the API and on screen.

## Scope

| ID | Rule | Decision |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Q-01 | BR-20, GR-06 | A visitor gets 401 on any action that requires an account; 403 only for a signed-in user with the wrong account type |
| Q-03 | BR-18, GR-02 | A job's requirements with only spaces are rejected with 422 "This field can't contain only spaces" |
| N-15 | FR-31, FR-38 | Dates are shown in Brazil time (America/Sao_Paulo), whatever the viewer's time zone |
| N-24 | BR-19 | `/vaga-antiga/{id}` redirects only a whole id from 1 up; anything else shows the 404 page |
| — | BR-29 | The company's average always rounds up to 1 decimal place |
| — | BR-33 | One review per candidate per job; after deleting it, the candidate can review again |
| — | FR-31, FR-38 | Dates use the date format of the browser's language, still in Brazil time |

## Results

| ID | Result | Evidence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| BR-20 | Confirmed | `POST /api/jobs/1/apply` without a session returned 401 `not_authenticated`, and with an invalid session 401 `invalid_session` (before the fix: 403). A signed-in company got 403 `forbidden` "Only "candidate" accounts can access this". A candidate got 201 with status `received`; applying again returned 409 `already_applied`, and applying to the closed job 14 returned 409 `job_closed`. On screen, a visitor who clicked "Apply now" on job 1 went to `/pages/entrar.html?reason=apply` and saw "Sign in with a candidate account to apply". |
| BR-18 | Confirmed | `POST /api/jobs` with requirements `"   "` returned 422 with field `requirements` and "This field can't contain only spaces", and no job was created. `PUT /api/jobs/:id` with `"    "` returned 422 and kept the previous value. `"  Node.js  "` was saved as "Node.js" and `" Express "` as "Express"; a job without requirements and a `PUT` with `""` were accepted (before the fix: only spaces were saved as `""`). On the company's job form, requirements with only spaces showed the same message as an error toast, and no request was sent. |
| FR-31 | Confirmed | The seed review created on `2026-06-01T00:00:00Z` showed "5/31/2026" on the job page, and the reviews of 2026-07-15T00:00Z and 2026-06-01T00:00Z showed "7/14/2026" and "5/31/2026" on the Nimbus Tech profile; in UTC the same dates would be 7/15/2026 and 6/1/2026. The pages format the date with the time zone `America/Sao_Paulo`, so the result does not depend on the computer's time zone (the browser used was itself in America/Sao_Paulo, so the independence was confirmed in the code). |
| FR-38 | Confirmed in the code | Job activity formats "applied on {date}" with the time zone `America/Sao_Paulo`, the same as the reviews; no application was created for this check. |
| BR-19 | Confirmed | `/vaga-antiga/14` and `/vaga-antiga/1` returned 302, and `/vaga-antiga/14` landed on `/pages/vaga.html?id=14`. `/vaga-antiga/abc`, `0`, `-1`, `1.5`, and `01` returned 404 with the page "404 — Page not found" (before the fix, any value was redirected). |
| BR-29 | Confirmed | Grain Foods (no reviews) received 4, 5, and 4 stars from three test candidates on job 3: the average went from null to 4, 4.5, and 4.4, and the job page showed "4.4 out of 5 (3 reviews)" (before the fix, 4.3). An exact average of 4.3 (ten ratings) stays 4.3, checked with the same formula before the change. |
| BR-33 | Confirmed | A second review of job 3 by the same candidate returned 409 `already_reviewed` "You have already reviewed this job"; a review of another job (job 2) returned 201. On the page of job 3, that candidate saw the message and no form. After deleting the review (204), the form came back and a new review returned 201. |
| FR-31, FR-38 | Confirmed | With the browser in English (US), the review dates showed "11/4/2026", the same as the browser's own format in Brazil time. The four pages format the date with the browser's language and the time zone `America/Sao_Paulo`, with no fixed language left in the code. The same date of `2026-06-01T00:00Z` formats as 5/31/2026 in English (US) and 31/05/2026 in Portuguese (Brazil); a browser in Portuguese was not available, so that case was checked with the same formatter, not on screen. |
| QA guide | Confirmed | `/pages/qa-docs.html` showed "Not signed in (401)", "Permissions (403)", "401 when logged out" on the apply row, the split `/api/jobs` and `/api/jobs/:id` rows, and the five added routes; after fixes 4 and 5, the rows "Dates in Brazil time" and the redirect row with `/vaga-antiga/abc`; after fixes 6 to 8, the rounding rule, `already_reviewed`, and the browser's date format. |
