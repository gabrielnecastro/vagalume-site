### Observed behavior that was not documented

- The API search does not remove spaces around the term: `q=%20qa%20` returns no jobs. The screen removes them before sending (`q=qa`, 1 job). Decided on 2026-10-17: defect ([N-04](../../divergences.md#new-findings-during-validation)); the API must ignore the spaces, as searches usually do. BR-13 was updated. Fixed and validated on 2026-10-17.
- A job whose requirements contain only spaces shows an empty "Requirements" section instead of "Not specified.". Decided on 2026-10-17: defect ([N-05](../../divergences.md#new-findings-during-validation)); when the job is saved the spaces are removed and the job is saved with empty requirements, and the page shows "Not specified." for empty or only-spaces requirements. BR-18 was updated. Fixed and validated on 2026-10-17.
- Out-of-range pagination values are corrected silently, never rejected: `limit=101` becomes 100 and `limit=-5` becomes 1, but `limit=0` becomes 10 (the default). `page` of 0, negative, or text becomes 1; `page=99` returns 200 with an empty list. Decided on 2026-10-17: defect ([N-06](../../divergences.md#new-findings-during-validation)); out-of-range or non-numeric `limit` and `page` must return 422, and a page after the last one keeps returning 200 with an empty list. BR-16 was updated. Fixed and validated on 2026-10-17.

- Job and company text is inserted into the cards as HTML: a job with the title `<b data-qa="html-job">QA Html Job</b>`, from a company named `<i data-qa="html-co">QA Html Co</i>`, was shown in bold and italics on the home page, and the API saved the tags. Any visitor of the job list sees it. Decided on 2026-10-17: defect ([N-21](../../divergences.md#new-findings-during-validation)), the same decision as N-11. Fixed and validated on 2026-10-17.

All three points were decided on 2026-10-17 as defects N-04 to N-06 and fixed the same day; N-21 was found and fixed later that day.
